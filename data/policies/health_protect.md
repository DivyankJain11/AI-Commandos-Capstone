# Northwind Health Protect

Product: Health

Synthetic wording written for this capstone. It is not a real insurance contract.

## HP-01 Sum insured

The Sum Insured is the annual limit shown on the schedule. All admissible claims in a policy year are paid up to the Sum Insured. A recharge of 100 percent of the Sum Insured is available once per policy year after the Sum Insured is exhausted, and the recharge applies only to a subsequent illness.

## HP-02 Initial waiting period

Expenses for an illness are not payable during the first 30 days from policy inception. Treatment of accidental injuries is covered from day one. This initial waiting period does not apply on renewal when the policy is renewed without a break.

## HP-03 Pre-existing disease

Pre-existing diseases and their direct complications are covered only after 36 continuous months of coverage. A pre-existing disease means a condition that was diagnosed or treated during the 36 months before the first policy start date.

## HP-04 Specific disease waiting period

Cataract, hernia, joint replacement, and sinusitis are covered only after 24 continuous months. Claims for these conditions before 24 months are not payable.

## HP-05 Co-pay

A 10 percent co-pay applies to each admissible claim when the insured person is 61 years or older on the date of admission. Co-pay is waived for accidental injury claims.

## HP-06 Room rent

Room rent is limited to 1 percent of the Sum Insured per day for a normal room and 2 percent of the Sum Insured per day for an intensive care unit. Related expenses are reduced in the same proportion if a higher room category is chosen.

## HP-07 Day care

Listed day-care procedures are covered even when the hospital stay is less than 24 hours. The procedure must appear on the day-care list attached to the policy.

## HP-08 Maternity

Maternity expenses are covered only when the maternity add-on is selected and only after a waiting period of 36 months from the add-on start date. The sub-limit is Rs 50,000 for a normal delivery and Rs 75,000 for a caesarean section. Newborn cover is limited to the first 90 days and sits inside that sub-limit.

## HP-09 Ambulance

Road ambulance charges are payable up to Rs 2,000 per hospitalisation when the admission itself is an admissible claim.

## HP-10 AYUSH

In-patient treatment in a registered Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, or Homeopathy hospital is covered up to the Sum Insured. Outpatient AYUSH consultations are not covered.

## HP-11 Domiciliary treatment

Home treatment is covered only when the attending doctor certifies that the patient cannot be moved to a hospital, or when no hospital bed is available. Domiciliary treatment is capped at 10 percent of the Sum Insured. Pre-hospitalisation and post-hospitalisation expenses are not paid under domiciliary treatment.

## HP-12 Geography

Treatment is covered only in India. Emergency hospitalisation outside India is not covered under this product.
