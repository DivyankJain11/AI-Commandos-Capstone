# Northwind Private Car wording

Product: Motor

Synthetic wording written for this capstone. It is not a real insurance contract.

## MC-01 Own damage

Own Damage cover pays for accidental loss or damage to the insured car, subject to the deductible and to depreciation, up to the Insured Declared Value.

## MC-02 Insured declared value

The Insured Declared Value is the current market value of the car agreed at inception. It is the maximum payable on a total loss or a theft claim.

## MC-03 Compulsory deductible

A compulsory deductible of Rs 1,000 applies to each Own Damage claim for a car up to 1500cc. A compulsory deductible of Rs 2,000 applies to each Own Damage claim for a car above 1500cc.

## MC-04 Third party

Third-party property damage is covered up to Rs 7,50,000 per accident. Third-party bodily injury is covered as required by the Motor Vehicles Act, and this wording states no separate policy sub-limit for that bodily injury.

## MC-05 No claim bonus

No Claim Bonus on the Own Damage premium is 20 percent after one claim-free year, 25 percent after two, 35 percent after three, 45 percent after four, and 50 percent after five or more claim-free years. An Own Damage claim reduces the bonus to zero at the next renewal. A third-party-only claim does not reduce No Claim Bonus.

## MC-06 Metal depreciation

For a partial loss, depreciation on metal parts is nil during the first 6 months, 5 percent after 6 months up to 1 year, 10 percent after 1 year up to 2 years, and 15 percent after 2 years up to 3 years.

## MC-07 Plastic glass and tyres depreciation

Rubber, nylon, plastic parts, and tyres that are replaced are subject to 50 percent depreciation. Glass is subject to nil depreciation.

## MC-08 Tyre only damage

Damage to tyres and tubes is not covered unless the car also suffers other accidental damage in the same event and an Own Damage claim is payable for that other damage.

## MC-09 Consumables

Engine oil, lubricants, nuts and bolts, and similar consumables are not covered unless the consumable add-on has been purchased.

## MC-10 Zero depreciation add-on

The zero depreciation add-on waives depreciation on metal and plastic parts for a car not older than 5 years. It applies only to the first two partial-loss claims in a policy year. The tyre-only exclusion is unchanged by this add-on.

## MC-11 Engine protect add-on

The engine protect add-on covers repair or replacement of the engine, gearbox, and differential when the damage is caused by water ingression, including hydrostatic lock, or by leakage of lubricating oil. Normal wear is not covered under this add-on.

## MC-12 Key protect add-on

The key-protect add-on pays up to Rs 25,000 in a policy year to replace car keys and lock sets after a break-in or after theft of the keys. Keys that were lost without a break-in or a theft are not covered.
