# Northwind Health Protect exclusions

Product: Health

Synthetic wording written for this capstone. It is not a real insurance contract.

## EX-01 Adventure sports

Treatment of injuries from adventure sports, including mountaineering, paragliding, and motor racing, is excluded.

## EX-02 Cosmetic surgery

Cosmetic or aesthetic surgery is excluded. Reconstructive surgery required after an accident that is otherwise admissible is not excluded by this clause.

## EX-03 Dental treatment

Dental treatment is excluded. Treatment required because of an accidental injury to natural teeth during the policy period is not excluded by this clause.

## EX-04 Obesity

Treatment of obesity, weight-control surgery, and complications arising primarily from obesity are excluded.

## EX-05 Infertility

Infertility treatment, IVF, and reversal of sterilisation are excluded.

## EX-06 War and nuclear risk

Injury or illness arising from war, invasion, or nuclear contamination is excluded.

## EX-07 Unproven treatment

Treatments that are unproven or experimental, or that are not recognised by standard medical practice in India, are excluded.

## EX-08 Self-inflicted injury

Treatment of a self-inflicted injury and treatment following an attempted suicide are excluded.
