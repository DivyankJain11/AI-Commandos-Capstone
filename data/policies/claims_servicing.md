# Northwind claims servicing

Product: Claims

Synthetic wording written for this capstone. It is not a real insurance contract.

## CL-01 Intimation

A planned health admission must be intimated at least 48 hours before admission. An emergency health admission must be intimated within 24 hours after admission. A motor accident must be intimated within 48 hours. Theft of the vehicle must be intimated within 24 hours of discovery.

## CL-02 Cashless approval

Cashless treatment is available only at a network hospital. A pre-authorisation request must include the claim form, photo identity, the policy number, and the hospital clinical notes. Approval authorises the hospital. It is not a promise that every line item is payable.

## CL-03 Reimbursement documents

A reimbursement health claim requires the claim form, the discharge summary, the original itemised bills, the investigation reports, a cancelled cheque, and identity proof. A motor Own Damage claim also requires a copy of the registration certificate, the driving licence, and an FIR when the loss is a theft or involves a third party.

## CL-04 Motor survey

A motor surveyor is appointed when the estimated Own Damage loss exceeds Rs 50,000. The insured must make the vehicle available for survey before repairs begin, except for minor work needed to move the vehicle to safety.

## CL-05 Settlement time

After the last required document is received, the company offers a settlement or a rejection with reasons within 15 working days for a health claim and within 21 working days for a motor claim.

## CL-06 When a claim can be rejected

A claim may be rejected when it falls under an exclusion, when a waiting period is not complete, when a material fact was withheld at proposal, or when the requested documents are not submitted within 30 days of the request and there is no acceptable reason for the delay.

## CL-07 Grievance

A complaint is first raised with the Grievance Redressal Officer. If it is still unresolved after 15 days, the insured may approach the Insurance Ombudsman for that jurisdiction. An Ombudsman complaint must be filed within one year of the rejection.

## CL-08 Fraud

When a claim is supported by a forged document or by a material false statement, the claim is forfeited and the policy may be cancelled. Premium is not refunded when fraud is proved.
